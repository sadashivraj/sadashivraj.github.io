        Visibility AI

        Proactive Executive Assistant on Webex

        🎯 Overview

        Directors and above lose hours every week reassembling the state of their portfolio from half a dozen tools. Visibility AI replaces that with one conversational surface on Webex — available on mobile — that federates their task tracker, mail, calendar, files, and meetings, and proactively tells them what changed while they weren't looking.

        🏗️ Architecture

        • Federated Tools: Six MCP servers — Asana, Microsoft 365 (Outlook/SharePoint), Airtable, Webex messaging and meetings — behind per-user OAuth
        • Ambient Engine: Webhook- and poll-driven change detection with watermark de-duplication, so users see only what is genuinely new
        • Security Model: Strict per-user token isolation; webhook payloads carry no data and are re-fetched with the user's own token, making forged payloads a no-op by design
        • Cost Tiering: Templated cards by default, escalating to LLM summaries only when the change set is large or important
        • Artifact Generation: A Deck Designer sub-agent plans deck structure in an isolated context window, then composes PPTX/XLSX/DOCX/PDF under machine-enforced brand constraints
